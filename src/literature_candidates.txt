## Cross-domain literature search for GSL stopping mechanisms

### Candidates not yet used in GSL:

1. **Testing by Betting** (Ramdas et al. 2023, Statistical Science)
   - E-processes via wealth processes in sequential betting
   - More general than simple exponential e-values
   - Could construct optimal e-values for GSL posterior dynamics

2. **Bayesian Optimal Stopping** (Chernoff 1961, Berry & Fristedt 1985)
   - Classical theory: minimize expected cost = search cost + error cost
   - MAP residual threshold as stopping rule is a special case
   - Could derive optimal threshold θ from cost structure

3. **Sequential Change Detection** (Tartakovsky et al. 2014)
   - CUSUM / Shiryaev-Roberts for detecting posterior convergence
   - CUSUM on MAP residuals could detect when convergence happens
   - More sensitive than simple threshold

4. **Information-Geometric Stopping** (Amari 2016)
   - Natural gradient descent on statistical manifold
   - Posterior convergence measured by Fisher information metric
   - Geodesic distance between consecutive posteriors

5. **Safe Testing** (Grünwald 2024)
   - Safe e-values: E[E_t | H0] = 1 exactly (not just <= 1)
   - More powerful than conservative e-values
   - Could improve detection speed

6. **Posterior Predictive Checking** (Box 1980, Rubin 1984)
   - Use predictive distribution p(z_{t+1} | z_{1:t}) for stopping
   - When posterior is well-calibrated, predictive density is high
   - Natural connection to Bayesian framework

7. **Bayesian Adaptive Design** (Chaloner & Verdinelli 1995)
   - Choose measurements to maximize information gain
   - Connection between design efficiency and stopping time
   - Could derive optimal stopping from design perspective

8. **Universal Inference** ( Wasserman et al. 2020)
   - Split data into training and testing
   - No exchangeability assumption needed
   - But loses statistical power compared to e-processes
