# 12 Key Papers (Must-Read for Context)

## Tier 1: SOTA GSL Methods (Must Compare Against)

1. **PMFS** — Ojeda, Monroy, Gonzalez-Jimenez. "Robotic Gas Source Localization
   with Probabilistic Mapping and Online Dispersion Simulation."
   IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 40:3551-3564, 2024.
   arXiv:2304.08879 | GitHub: MAPIRlab/GasSourceLocalization

2. **GrGSL** — Ojeda, Monroy, Gonzalez-Jimenez. "Information-Driven Gas Source
   Localization Exploiting Gas and Wind Local Measurements for Autonomous Mobile
   Robots." IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, 6(2):1320-1326, 2021.

3. **Infotaxis** — Vergassola, Villermaux, Shraiman. "Infotaxis as a strategy
   for searching without gradients." Nature, 445:406-409, 2007.

4. **GSL-Bench** — Erwich, Duisterhof, de Croon. "GSL-Bench: High Fidelity
   Gas Source Localization Benchmarking Tool." ICRA, 2024.

## Tier 2: Recent UAV GSL Papers (Direct Competitors)

5. **3D Infotaxis+PF** — "Gas Source Localization for UAV Using Infotaxis-Based
   Three-Dimensional Algorithm and Particle Filtering." CCC, 2024.

6. **3D Entrotaxis** — "A 3D-Extended Entrotaxis Strategy for UAV Odor Source
   Localization in Turbulent Environments." CAC, 2025.

7. **RL-UAV** — "Reinforcement Learning-Based Autonomous UAV Navigation for
   CO Source Localization." IEEE Sensors Letters, 2025.

8. **PF-WOA** — "A novel hybrid information-based PF-WOA algorithm for gas
   source localization in 3D space." Robotica, 2024.

## Tier 3: Cross-Domain Methods (Innovation Sources)

9. **PGNN-GSL** — Ruiz et al. "Gas Source Localization Using Physics-Guided
   Neural Networks." ISOEN, 2024. (7 citations)

10. **PGNN Sparse GSL** — "Physics-Guided Neural Networks for Distributed
    Sparse Gas Source Localization." EUSIPCO, 2024.

11. **SniffySquad** — "Patchiness-Aware Gas Source Localization with Multi-Robot
    Collaboration." ACM TOSN, 2024.

12. **Belief Clustering** — Jin & Meger. "Sense in Motion with Belief
    Clustering: Efficient GSL with Mobile Robots." ICRA, 2024.

## Tier 4: Datasets and Benchmarks

14. **VGR Dataset** — Ojeda, Monroy, Gonzalez-Jimenez. "VGR dataset: A

15. **PSGSL** — Ojeda, Monroy, Gonzalez-Jimenez. "PSGSL: A Probabilistic
    Framework Integrating Semantic Scene Understanding and Gas Sensing."
    arXiv:2501.12812, 2025.
