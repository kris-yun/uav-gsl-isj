# Why RIA-A0 exists

The independent SPX review showed that FULL-vs-BP/MBD mean NLL can reverse while accuracy and Brier move differently, and that a single catastrophic target can dominate a pair/panel mean.

Therefore `model utility` is not a safe surrogate for `physical source information`.

RIA-A0 separates:

1. source-conditioned observation-distribution separation estimated from reference observations only;
2. bounded heldout operational discriminability;
3. estimator-specific wrong-confidence/tail risk.

This is intentionally a pre-admission audit.

No biological/physics mother theory is authorized unless a stable observation-level identifiability target survives this separation.
