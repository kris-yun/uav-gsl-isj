# OCB-R2 R2 broad-memory path factor decision

**Decision: `OCB_R2_R2_MZ_PATH_FACTOR_DISCOVERY_ADVANCE`.** Both frozen discovery gates passed. This licenses human consideration of a new multi-source prospective discovery panel; it is not method confirmation, full-map localization improvement, or proof of a Mori–Zwanzig equation.

## Frozen data and repeat

The R0/R1 nine input-contract files, 64 `10×30` pooled tensor hashes, and the R1 deterministic repeat all passed exact parity. Encounter was `concentration > 0`; no target was re-extracted. Two full R2 scoring passes were byte-identical in all ten scientific output files. Independent finalization recomputed all 256 exported candidate margins, 64 `D_MZ` contrasts, House medians, and both 256-configuration exact context sign-flip references. The Q-time audit passed **960,000** surrogate reference views and **7,680,000** pair-preservation assertions.

## Gate A: projected-history predictive memory

The preregistered H3–H1 truth-source predictive-gain contrast passed:

The inherited parameter-free diagnostic evaluates H1 over prediction times `t=0..8` and H3 over `t=2..8`; therefore this frozen contrast contains a change in eligible prediction times as well as history length. It supports the stated exploratory physics gate, but by itself does not isolate a causal memory kernel or prove non-Markovian closure.

## Gate B: source-conditioned broad-memory path evidence

For each candidate source and each lag 1–3, the actual candidate term was `median(ES_Q_s,L)-ES_RAW_s,L`; three lags were averaged equally before taking truth minus alternative. The Q-time shuffles preserved both full 30-D time-specific snapshot multisets. Gate B passed:

|---|---:|---:|
| H01 median group `Delta_BM` | +0.012868 | >0 |
| H02 median group `Delta_BM` | +0.010856 | >0 |
| Positive source×context groups | 16/16 | ≥12/16 |
| Positive contexts | 8/8 | ≥6/8 |
| Leave-one-context-out pooled medians | all positive | all positive |
| One-sided exact 8-context sign-flip | 0.00390625 | ≤0.05 |
| Largest absolute context contribution | 30.05% (X00) | ≤40% |

All four alternative-source 3-of-4 omission views retained positive H01, H02 and pooled group medians. Their positive group counts were **15, 16, 14, 15/16**. The smallest House median among those views was +0.005959.

## Descriptive complementarity

R0 M-FULL already classified **64/64** two-source discovery targets, so there is no accuracy headroom in this panel. Across the 16 source×context groups, Spearman between `Delta_BM` and M-FULL margin was **−0.3471**. In the bottom baseline-margin quartile, `Delta_BM` was positive for **14/16** targets, with mean **+0.026445**. All eight groups in each of the lower and upper M-FULL group halves had positive mean `Delta_BM`. Both fast and slow strata, gas10 and gas13 strata, and both Houses had positive group effects. These are descriptive observations and did not enter either gate.

## Formula and inference boundary

The protocol calls the candidate-specific formula algebraically equal to R1's paired median difference. Medians do not generally distribute over subtraction. The frozen operational rule used the explicitly defined candidate-specific `E_s,L` formula. Compared with R1's paired-median `I_LAG` averaged over lags 1–3, 1/64 primary targets was numerically equal within `1e-12`; the maximum absolute difference was **0.003909**. This difference was audited, not tuned, and it did not change the R2 formula or gate.

This is a matched **two-source-per-context discovery** result. The broad-memory term is an Energy Score contrast, not a normalized source likelihood or calibrated PMFS posterior. Its contribution to complete source-map localization and performance on unseen sources or House03 remains untested. H01/H02 confirmation and House03 stayed sealed. No GADEN, PMFS forward, learned model, literature search, or closed loop was run.
