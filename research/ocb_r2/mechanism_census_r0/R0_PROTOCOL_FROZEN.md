# OCB-R2 mechanism census R0 execution freeze

The scientific protocol is [`OCB_R2_SOURCE_INFORMATION_MECHANISM_CENSUS_R0.md`](../OCB_R2_SOURCE_INFORMATION_MECHANISM_CENSUS_R0.md) at parent commit `85cd41cb136be08e440d26f8cf8f36a5d35b852c`. This file fixes implementation choices **before R0 scores are calculated**. It does not alter the scientific tests or thresholds.

- Use only the 64 SHA-checked AOD-R2 F0 pooled H01/H02 discovery tensors. Threshold concentration strictly at `>0`. S2 and S2X runlists define the eight matched contexts and four replicate indices per source.
- Same-index primary leave-one-out uses replicate index `r` for both source banks. Alternative omission robustness evaluates all four alternative omitted indices with the truth bank unchanged.
- `M-TIME`, `M-SPACE`, and `M-FULL` use squared error mean over their respective 10, 30, and 300 coordinates. Positive margin always means `score_alternative − score_truth > 0`.
- Fair Energy Score for K=3 is `mean_i ||X_i−y||_2 − [sum_{i<j} ||X_i−X_j||_2]/[K(K−1)]`, flattening the complete binary `10×30` tensor. `G_C1[b]` and `G_C2[b]` are **paired margin differences at the same surrogate draw index**, and the median is taken over these 1,000 margins, not separately over two source scores.
- For each context/source/target replicate/reference omission, C1 and C2 RNG streams are keyed via NumPy `SeedSequence([seed, context_index, source_index, target_replicate_index, omitted_reference_index])`, with seeds `2026093001` and `2026093002`. A C1 draw independently permutes the K source-realization labels at every coordinate. A C2 draw independently permutes K complete 30D snapshots at every time. Permutation arrays are drawn by `argsort` of iid uniforms, stably ordered for deterministic replay. The corresponding preservation assertions are checked for **all 1,000 draws**.
- Source-label null: within each of the eight contexts, swap both source-group labels together (one sign per context); 10,000 sign vectors use `default_rng(2026093003)`. The pooled directional statistic is the mean of 16 source×context **group means**. The one-sided randomization reference is `(1 + #null >= observed)/(10001)`. This is a randomization reference, not a universal p-value.
- Gate hierarchy uses source×context **group mean** effects; House values are medians of its eight group means. A context value is the mean of its two group means. Leave-one-context-out pooled value is the median of the remaining 14 group means. The 40% concentration check uses `max_context |context_mean| / sum_context |context_mean|` (zero denominator fails). The randomization reference and this contribution are evaluated for the nominated I_CROSS or I_SPATIAL effect. Omission robustness recomputes the entire aggregate effect and requires both House medians and pooled median to keep a positive sign for each of the four alternative omissions.
- For `M-FULL` stability, require both House medians of eight group means >0, at least 12/16 positive group means, and at least 6/8 positive context means. If neither joint gate passes and this M-FULL condition holds, report `MARGINAL_DOMINANT`. If neither joint gate passes, M-FULL is not stable, and both House medians are nonpositive, report `OBSERVATION_INSUFFICIENT`. Otherwise mixed evidence is `INCONSISTENT_HOLD`. M-TIME/M-SPACE descriptions cannot change the mechanism label.
- If M-FULL wins all 64 two-source comparisons, report the ceiling explicitly. The bottom quartile by target M-FULL margin is descriptive only.
- Determinism gate: execute the full scorer twice from the same frozen inputs; all scientific result files must match byte-for-byte. A failed assertion or mismatch is an infrastructure HOLD, not a science label.
