# OCB-R2 R1 execution freeze

The scientific protocol is `research/ocb_r2/OCB_R2_CROSS_TIME_MEMORY_ANATOMY_R1.md` at source commit `a715dd2f198a0f55e2cef78c3b017ef97319a733`, incorporated here without changing its content. This file fixes implementation choices before any R1 score is computed.

- Input parity compares every file and all 64 pooled tensor SHA256 values in R0 `R0_INPUT_HASHES.json`, then verifies the R0 target effect file against its deterministic repeat manifest. No concentration is re-extracted. Binary encounter is exactly `pooled_concentration > 0`.
- Each lag is a **pair-specific** intervention. At a fixed lag and valid `t`, the first 30D reference snapshot keeps its realization label; only the second 30D snapshot is independently permuted. A time slot can appear in different roles across pairs, so the lag score averages separate 60D pair forecasts; it is not presented as one globally consistent surrogate trajectory.
- Every intervention uses K=3 truth and alternative references, the R0 fair-U Energy Score, and a median of **paired source-margin differences** over 1,000 surrogate draws. The intact lag score averages 60D fair-U pair scores over valid `t` before forming the source margin.
- For reproducible independent candidate/reference views, each lag RNG is `SeedSequence([2026093100+L, context_index, source_index, target_replicate_index, omitted_reference_index])`; BLOCK-2 and BLOCK-5 use the same key structure with seeds `2026093120` and `2026093150`. The permutation is the stable `argsort` of iid uniforms. All 1,000 draws are checked for exact K, binary, marginal, and prescribed pair/block multiset preservation.
- BLOCK-1 and BLOCK-10 are R0 C2 and RAW **margins**, not scores regenerated under a new seed. For the primary same-index omission, the recomputed margins must match R0's saved target effects to `1e-12`. For all alternative omissions, the frozen R0 C2 generator is run with its original seed `2026093002` to supply the corresponding B1 margin; B10 is recomputed with the unchanged fair-U formula.
- Each target with a strictly positive R0 cross-time denominator reports unmodified `R_B2` and `R_B5`; otherwise its ratios are undefined and are not forced to zero. For each source×context group, recovery uses the **group-mean margin increment** divided by its group-mean R0 cross-time increment. The House recovery statistic is the median of its eight group recovery ratios. A nonpositive group denominator makes that House's gate fail; no ratio is clipped.
- A lag is called directionally stable if its I_LAG House medians are both positive and at least 12/16 source×context group means are positive. For the BROAD lag branch, require the **same lag L≥3** to remain stable under all four alternative reference omissions. The alternative BLOCK-5 branch requires both Houses to recover at least 2/3 while BLOCK-2 is below 2/3 in both Houses for the primary and all omissions.
- SHORT requires the lag-1 directional gate and BLOCK-2 House recovery ≥2/3 for the primary **and each of four omissions**. WHOLE requires neither SHORT nor BROAD, B5 recovery <2/3 in at least one House for the primary and each omission, and positive R0 cross-time House medians under each omission. Otherwise label HOLD. Decision order is SHORT → BROAD → WHOLE → HOLD. No lag or block size is selected post hoc for a method.
- Source×context is the primary aggregation unit. Context means average its two groups; House values are medians of its eight group means; leave-one-context-out values are medians of the remaining 14 group means. Time pairs are never scientific replicates. A second complete pass must reproduce all scientific files byte-for-byte; failure is infrastructure HOLD.
