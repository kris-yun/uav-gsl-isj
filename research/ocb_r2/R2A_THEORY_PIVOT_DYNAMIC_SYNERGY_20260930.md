## 2. Revised mother-theory candidate

### Dynamic synergistic information / information-rate decomposition

A more direct mother theory is the information-theoretic idea that a target can
be encoded **synergistically** across multiple temporally structured
observations: information exists in their joint dependence that is not
available from time slices treated independently.

Relevant recent distant-field theory:

- **Partial Information Rate Decomposition (PIRD), Physical Review Letters,
  2025**: extends partial-information decomposition from memoryless random
  variables to temporally correlated random processes and decomposes dynamic
  information into unique, redundant and synergistic contributions.
- **Sensory PID, ICML 2026**: uses conditional partial-information
  decomposition plus modality-shuffling interventions to identify synergistic
  decision information in multimodal systems.

This is conceptually closer to the OCB-R2 evidence than an MZ predictive
closure: R2A showed that naive broad-history next-step prediction is not stable,
whereas candidate-wise path dependence remains 16/16 positive.

## 3. GSL-specific second innovation

Do not claim to invent PID, total correlation, copulas, or temporal dependence.

The proposed GSL-specific scientific object is:

> **Source-Conditioned Temporal Synergistic Path Evidence**

For source candidate s and trajectory Y:

`Q_s(Y_1:T) = product_t P_s(Y_t)`

where each Y_t is the complete spatial snapshot block.

Define the dependence/path factor:

`R_s(Y_1:T) = P_s(Y_1:T) / Q_s(Y_1:T)`

and pointwise path evidence:

`e_s(Y) = log R_s(Y)`.

The key GSL adaptation is that the independence reference preserves the
complete time-specific spatial snapshot law and destroys only cross-time
realization coupling.

Thus the new term is not a second-stage reranker. It is a decomposition of
source evidence into:

`source evidence = snapshot/marginal evidence + temporal-synergy path evidence`
