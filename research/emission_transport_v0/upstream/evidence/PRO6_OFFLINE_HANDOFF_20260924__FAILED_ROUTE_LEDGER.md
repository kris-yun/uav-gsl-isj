# Failed / Stopped Route Ledger — Do Not Restart

## TNQC v5 — STOP/NO-GO
Projection/quotient nuisance removal produced near-zero or slightly negative gains across six H01/H02/H03 cases. Lesson: algebraic nuisance removal is insufficient when source and nuisance information are entangled.

## Active Deconfounding — NO-GO
Equal-budget intervention/deconfounding experiment: 0/6 pass. Do not restart as generic Bayesian/active experimental design.

## DRPE — retired
NeurIPS 2025 posterior/reweighting route. Similar ideas exist in olfaction; insufficient novelty and reference behavior not load-bearing.

## MIPO Active Observability — retired
Specific observability point had relevant prior art. Do not rerun merely because active sensing sounds attractive.

## HCMC v1 — `HCMC_V1_INDEPENDENT_OFFLINE_NO_GO`
Old/offline data looked promising; fresh independent data did not. Key lesson: fresh validation is mandatory.

## Dynamic export/parity — STOP
Historical intermediate wind grids were overwritten; exact parity could not be established. Do not build a main claim on unrecoverable historical state.

## M4-v2 — HOLD
Operator field prediction improved, but source ranking did not. Better field prediction is not sufficient.

## M4-v3 — `D0_FAIL_STOP_M4_V3`
Wind-response cosine too low, source superposition error too high, one field-error comparison worse. No rescue.

## Source-Lineage Lagrangian v2 — `L1_FAIL_STOP_SOURCE_LINEAGE_MAINLINE`
Deterministic 3D physics reached the simulator stochastic floor; learned lineage residual added only ~0–2%. Do not add another deterministic learner at the noise floor.

## Causal Biorthogonal Green — `GATE1A_FAIL_STOP_SOURCE_TO_SENSOR_GREEN_FAMILY`
Exact deterministic source→sensor physics gave S2 A/B truth ranks 1/7. It identified the local basin but exact 0.30 m cell identity was realization-sensitive. Neural Green cannot rescue an oracle-level failure.

## Mori–Zwanzig realization-invariant source inference — `D3_FAIL_STOP_MZ_SOURCE_INFERENCE_MAINLINE`
S2 normalization+memory looked strong, but fresh S1 showed that global mass removal deleted useful source information. Global invariance was falsified.

## PASI path-action — `PASI_D0_FAIL_STOP_PATH_ACTION_MAINLINE`
Source-conditioned diagonal Gaussian/path-action proxy fit discovery cases but failed fresh S3: E 4/3/3/3, F 13/14/13/13. More importantly, S3 exposed that C/D two-sample stochasticity labels were badly under-sampled.

## Permanent lessons

- Do not equate better plume field prediction with better source localization.
- Do not turn two stochastic realizations into a physical law.
- Do not use global nuisance removal when the same observable can be signal in one source region and nuisance in another.
- Do not rename ordinary statistical scores with a physics term and call that a main innovation.
- Future gates must separate source-basin identifiability, exact-cell identifiability, and probability-map calibration.
