# RPO scientific boundary

RIA-A0 is the prerequisite that makes RPO scientifically admissible.

RIA showed that reference-only D_CNR and normalized energy distance track independent heldout bounded discrimination changes, while those quantities are nearly unrelated to JTD/FULL NLL utility.

Therefore RPO predicts observation-level identifiability, not model success/failure.

Biological sensorimotor-contingency and context-dependent sensory-gating theories are provisional conceptual anchors only.

2025 work on sensory consequences of self-action emphasizes that sensory gating/state estimation depend on statistical regularities of action-related sensing.

2026 work on flexible primary sensory cortex emphasizes context-dependent behaviorally relevant sensory representations.

2026 PRX Life work shows flow physically reformats odor signals between source and sensor.

None of these papers establishes the proposed GSL relation.

RPO-G0 must show that relational source–probe–flow context predicts the RIA identifiability target beyond geometry and simple wind heuristics before any mother-theory claim is allowed.
