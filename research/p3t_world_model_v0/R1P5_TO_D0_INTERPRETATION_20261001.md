# R1P5 interpretation and transition to P3T-D0 — 2026-10-01

Parent result: `PMFS3D_R1P5_HOLD_PARTIAL_SELECTIVITY`.

## What R1P5 established

R1P5 ruled out the narrow explanation that Oracle-3D only happened to depress one current best-wrong source.

Across the four frozen historical cases, among wrong candidates that originally outranked the truth in Oracle-2D:
- selective suppression passed in 4/4 cases;
- positive pairwise truth-advantage fractions were 78.82% to 98.95%;
- median pairwise truth-advantage gains were all positive.

Therefore 3-D transport contains broad candidate-discriminative information.

## What R1P5 did not establish

The suppression pattern is not globally stable enough to become a source-hypothesis elimination mechanism:
- House01 cross-seed suppression Spearman = -0.136917;
- House02 = +0.570227;
- House01/seed0 repaired 13 previously-ahead wrong candidates but created 32 new harmful crossings;
- all four cases added truth ties and removed none.

Thus the current point-hit Oracle-3D operator does not provide a stable false-source ordering across observations/environments.

Decision:
- keep the scientific fact that 3-D transport contains broad discriminative information;
- do not promote counterexample-guided elimination;
- do not build a new scorer around R1P5;
- move to the representation question: can a physically continuous 3-D gas support restore positive evidence at the truth?

## Why P3T-D0 is the correct next experiment

R1/R1P5 reveal a specific asymmetry:

1. 3-D changes many false-source scores;
2. the true-source score changes by exactly zero in all four cases;
3. the true template has zero support on confident observation cells;
4. point/voxel hit semantics create larger tie plateaus.

This pattern is consistent with a representation bottleneck: xyz center transport exists, but the observation operator is too sparse to convert physically nearby plume mass into source evidence.

D0 therefore changes only the gas-support representation, not the transport law, scorer, candidates, observations or source map output.

## Additional control required after R1P5

A positive Gaussian result alone would be ambiguous: wider/continuous support might improve source rank even in a collapsed 2-D transport model.

Therefore D0 must compare both:

- Gaussian support on frozen Oracle-2D trajectories; and
- the same Gaussian support on frozen Oracle-3D trajectories.

Only the second comparison can attribute any extra gain to retained 3-D transport rather than smoothing alone.

This control was added before D0 scientific execution.
