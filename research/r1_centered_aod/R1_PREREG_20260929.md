## Why this audit exists

R0.75 on 49 frozen H01/H02 Native trajectories established:

- CENTERED-B2 improves the House-level mean truth rank over ABS-B2 in all four
  House × {u, rawu} cells.
- REAL_ADJ does not add stable cross-House value beyond CENTERED.
- rawu and u become nearly indistinguishable at the House-mean level after
  centering (H01 90.19 vs 90.19; H02 66.68 vs 66.63).
- CENTERED still gives poor absolute ranks and no broad Top-1 recovery.

Therefore the next question is not whether relative coding is already a main
innovation. It is:

> Does the fresh House03 AOD benefit remain after the same common-mode
> centering, or do AOD and centering explain largely the same source evidence?

This is essential before treating AOD as an independent auxiliary innovation.

## Four frozen arms

For each target path observation y and candidate template m_s:

1. u-ABS:
   min_(g>=0) ||y - g m_s^u||^2

2. rawu-ABS:
   min_(g>=0) ||y - g m_s^rawu||^2

3. u-CENTERED:
   min_(g>=0) ||C y - g C m_s^u||^2

4. rawu-CENTERED:
   min_(g>=0) ||C y - g C m_s^rawu||^2

where C = I - (1/n)11^T for the ten frozen path stops.

No new EPS, weighting, path selection, source selection, candidate pruning,
temperature, posterior conversion, or threshold fitting.

CENTERED-B2 is algebraically equivalent to profiling an unconstrained additive
intercept b jointly with the existing nonnegative gain g:

  min_(g>=0,b) ||y - b1 - g m_s||^2.

This audit therefore tests a specific unmodeled common-mode/offset nuisance,
not a new action policy.

## Interpretation labels

Exactly one descriptive label:

### R1_CENTERING_NOT_CROSS_HOUSE
CENTERED does not reproduce the H01/H02 direction on House03 in either nominal
or stress at source-balanced rank, or materially damages the original primary
AOD endpoint. Stop relative/common-mode promotion.

### R1_CENTERING_DOMINANT_AOD_NOT_INCREMENTAL
CENTERED improves source-balanced rank versus ABS, but the original rawu-over-u
advantage largely collapses under CENTERED and no stable positive centered AOD
increment is supported. Interpret AOD and centering as overlapping mechanisms;
do not count AOD as an independent paper module.

### R1_CENTERING_AND_AOD_COMPLEMENTARY
CENTERED improves source-balanced rank and rawu retains a positive incremental
effect over u after centering, with the direction not concentrated in one
source only. This licenses keeping AOD as a separate auxiliary mechanism.

### R1_INCONCLUSIVE_MECHANISM
Mixed source/condition effects prevent either redundancy or complementarity
from being supported.

No label licenses VGR, probability fusion, PMFS repair, or a new network.
