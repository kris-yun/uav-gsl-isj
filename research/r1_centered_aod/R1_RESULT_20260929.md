# R1 Centering × AOD Orthogonality Audit

Decision: **`R1_CENTERING_NOT_CROSS_HOUSE`**.

This is the frozen read-only House03 F1 full624 audit. It uses the same 12 truth sources, eight realizations per source, two paths per realization, 624 candidates, and nominal/state0 template banks. No new plume, PMFS forward, training, ROS, or VGR run was made. The new bootstrap seed `2026092901` was committed before target scoring in `0357b74333a145d5b0e4bc0c76ce03ee880f0e15`.

In nominal conditions, centering raises mean truth rank by `19.706` for u and `10.169` for rawu. Under state0 it raises rank by `12.372` for u and `1.779` for rawu. These are adverse or neutral at the preregistered source-balanced rank endpoint, so the H01/H02 CENTERED direction does not transfer to House03. Nominal u rank improves for only 1/12 sources and rawu rank for 4/12; state0 counts are 2/12 and 6/12. Centering does not improve nominal Top1 in either operator, and state0 rawu Top1 falls by `0.0208`.

The AOD comparison remains descriptive: rawu versus u increases nominal unique Top1 by `0.0521` under ABS and `0.0573` under CENTERED. The corresponding state0 differences are `0.0469` and `0.0260`. However, centered rawu rank improves for 9/12 sources nominally but only 4/12 under state0, and centered source-balanced rank itself is worse than ABS. Thus this audit neither establishes common-mode correction as a cross-House method nor licenses the preregistered complementary-mechanism label. It also does not reverse the original frozen AOD F1 decision.

The 10,000 paired, source-stratified bootstrap draws kept both paths, all arms, and both conditions together within each resampled realization. Nominal centering rank-change 95% intervals were `[17.193, 22.136]` (u) and `[8.354, 11.940]` (rawu); positive values are worse. State0 intervals were `[9.117, 15.630]` (u) and `[-2.099, 5.518]` (rawu). These intervals describe plume variation for these fixed 12 sources, not a population of House03 sources.

The best-wrong B2 margin is reported only for u versus rawu within the same ABS or CENTERED transform. Raw SSE margins are not compared across transforms because centering changes the residual scale.

## Integrity and stop boundary

The full evaluator was run twice and the result JSON, target CSV, source CSV, bootstrap JSON, and asset manifest were byte-identical. SHA256 values are in `DETERMINISTIC_REPEAT.json` and `ASSET_MANIFEST_SHA256.tsv`. All 1,536 target-path-arm-condition rows are retained in `TARGET_METRICS.csv`.

R1 stops here. No VGR, planner, network, PMFS repair, or new mainline is authorized by this result.
