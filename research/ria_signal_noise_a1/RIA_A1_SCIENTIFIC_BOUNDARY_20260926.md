# Why RIA-A1 is necessary after RPO-G0 STOP

RPO-G0 showed that the hand-crafted source–probe–flow path descriptor does not prospectively predict RIA identifiability changes.

That does not invalidate RIA-A0.

Before trying another far-domain theory, decompose the supported identifiability target itself.

Preliminary posthoc inspection suggests probe-switch identifiability change may be driven mainly by between-source mean separation rather than within-source stochastic variability.

This preliminary observation is NOT a frozen result and must not be cited as a conclusion until RIA-A1 completes.

If confirmed, the branch becomes much closer to observation geometry/active observability and is unlikely to satisfy the project's main-innovation requirement without a genuinely new mechanism.
