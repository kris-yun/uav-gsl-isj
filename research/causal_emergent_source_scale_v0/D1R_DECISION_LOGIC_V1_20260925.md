# D1R Decision Logic V1

Date: 2026-09-25

D1R is reference development, not final confirmation.

## ADVANCE

D1R_ADVANCE_IFCV_STRONG_MAINLINE

Requires:
- nontrivial connected IF-CV partition;
- registered fidelity constraints non-vacuously satisfied;
- stability prerequisites satisfied;
- reference OOF candidate > identity;
- reference OOF candidate > locked ordinary champion;
- raw-amplitude and geometry audits show no invalid shortcut.

Action: freeze final model and D1C power plan; only then authorize untouched targets.

## HOLD

D1R_HOLD_WEAK_PREDICTIVE_POOLING_ONLY

Applies when pooling/shrinkage gives reproducible predictive improvement but strong fidelity/equivalence certification is vacuous or fails.

Interpretation: evidence for finite-sample pooling, not enough for intended causal-emergence-inspired main innovation.

Action: do not generate D1C mainline targets.

## STOP

D1R_STOP_FSEI_NOT_DISTINCT_FROM_ORDINARY_POOLING

Applies when identity or ordinary champion matches/beats candidate, candidate structure is unstable, mass/geometry integrity fails, or gains require unfair prior/task/model-budget changes.

Action: stop FSEI as mainline and return to mother-theory search using R0.
