## Why this task exists

XHOC-R0 found `XHOC_R0_COMMON_CONTRACT_PARTIAL`.

The E2 bank already gives a common cross-House acquisition operator:
- raw GADEN concentration;
- 30 source-blind virtual probes per House from the same geometry-only rule;
- 2x2 native-grid mean footprint at z=0.20 m;
- ten common saved-record IDs;
- no Native adaptive trajectory or stop averaging;
- six physical sources per House;
- four independent plume realizations/source.

The remaining problems are:
1. only 6 physical sources/House (<8 preregistered minimum);
2. only 4 realizations/source;
3. the record IDs 100..550 are not yet certified as physical simulator times / wind states;
4. existing H01/H02 sources have already been inspected scientifically, while H03 remains sealed.

The goal is to create a clean benchmark that can support:
DISCOVERY -> MECHANISM -> MOTHER THEORY -> UNTOUCHED CONFIRMATION.

## Stage A — time provenance gate (no scientific values)

1. Recover the writer-record mapping for the ten E2 record IDs
   100,150,...,550 for every relevant canonical environment.

## Stage C — new-run arithmetic

For each House under the chosen canonical environment:

Existing 6 sources currently have 4 realizations each.
Top them up with 4 NEW independent seeds/source:
  6 * 4 = 24 runs / House.

Two new sources require 8 independent seeds/source:
  2 * 8 = 16 runs / House.

Total:
  40 runs / House
  3 Houses
  = 120 NEW GADEN runs.

After completion:
  8 sources * 8 realizations = 64 runs / House,
  192 runs total in the canonical three-House benchmark
  (72 historical E2 canonical runs + 120 new runs).

This 120-run design is preferred over the arithmetic 24-run threshold top-up
because it simultaneously:
- reaches >=8 physical sources/House;
- reaches 8 realizations/source;
- creates 2 untouched source identities in H01 and H02;
- preserves all of H03 as an untouched external-House confirmation set.
