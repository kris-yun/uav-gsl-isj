# NOT EXECUTED — superseded by cross-House observation-contract audit

Date: 2026-09-29

The preregistered ORDINAL-BRIDGE D0 was prepared before the post-TCMA code and
contract audit identified that the existing H01/H02 and H03 evaluations use
different observation processes:

- H01/H02: Native action-dependent trajectories, completed-stop averaging,
  variable source-update prefixes;
- H03: two fixed paths with a different acquisition contract.

Although EDF/rank evidence is invariant to positive monotone measurement scale,
it does **not** remove this observation-process confound. Therefore executing
the prepared cross-House gate now would not establish a clean cross-House
mechanism.

Status:
- ORDINAL-BRIDGE D0: NOT EXECUTED / NOT AUTHORIZED.
- No scientific conclusion is attached to the unexecuted preregistration.
- The next activity is an observation-contract feasibility audit, not a
  rescoring rescue of TCMA.
