Use these papers primarily to learn **problem formulation and innovation derivation**, not to force-fit their algorithms into GSL.

5. MARFPNet: Multiattention and Adaptive Reparameterized Feature Pyramid Network for Small Target Detection on Water Surfaces.
6. Airborne Camera Dynamic Target Detection Based on Background Prediction and Semantic Compensation in Surface Environment.
7. A Strong UAV Vision Tracker Based on Deep Broad Learning System and Correlation Filter.
8. A Non-Parametric Scheme for Identifying Data Characteristic Based on Curve Similarity Matching.
9. 基于PCA和ICA模式融合的非高斯特征检测识别.
10. IMM-AIS-UKF: A GAN-Enhanced Dynamics-Constrained Trajectory Predictor With DDPG Method for UAV-USV Interaction.

Reading lens:
- What real scene creates the failure?
- Which assumption of the classical method breaks?
- How does each proposed module map one-to-one to that failure?
- What experiment rules out a simpler explanation?
- What are the stated validity boundaries?
- Which figures make the evidence chain obvious?

Do not claim every paper is directly about UAV gas localization; they are methodological/group-style foundations.
