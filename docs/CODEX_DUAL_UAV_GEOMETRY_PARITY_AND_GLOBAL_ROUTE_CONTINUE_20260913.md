At the frozen flight altitude, a center point `x0` is formation-feasible at the 2.0 m baseline if **there exists at least one baseline orientation** `theta` such that both receiver positions

\[

## G1.3 Source-blind formation orientation policy

For a feasible center point and current/past deployable local wind, select the baseline orientation by the deterministic rule:

