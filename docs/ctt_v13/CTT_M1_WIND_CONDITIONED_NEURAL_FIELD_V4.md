# CTT-M1 V4: Wind-Conditioned First-Passage Neural Field

## Motivation forced by G0

A response operator must be conditional on the physical state used to generate
it. House03 G0 showed that the same source and keyed stochastic member, evaluated
under two trajectory-dependent estimated wind fields, changes the 200-step hit
frequency by as much as 0.15 on the Free support. A single unconditioned per-House bank is therefore
not a valid cross-run forward operator.

## Distant-domain lineage and adaptation

The 2026 Nature Communications work *High-Rate Phase Association with Travel
Time Neural Fields* represents travel time continuously and conditions the
field on a latent encoding of the propagation medium. It uses the neural field
as a differentiable surrogate of a physical travel-time solver, rather than as
an unconstrained event classifier.

The 2026 Nature Communications framework *Assimilative causal inference*
formulates causal attribution in intermittent stochastic systems as a Bayesian
inverse problem: observed effects are assimilated through a governing model to
reduce uncertainty about possible causes.

CTT makes a second innovation rather than copying either method:

1. replace seismic wave speed with an online, trajectory-dependent wind and
   obstacle field;
2. replace deterministic wave arrival with a stochastic first-passage
   distribution including a never-arrived state;
3. infer candidate gas sources from an ordered binary burst-onset sequence;
4. factor the exact sequence likelihood into placement/order information and
   count/survival information for falsifiable module ablations.

## Variables

- `G`: frozen occupancy/geometry field;
- `W_b`: estimated wind field at source-update block `b`;
- `s`: candidate source coordinate;
- `x`: query/sensor cell;
- `k`: frozen stochastic transport member;
- `n`: native PMFS time bin;
- `F`: first-arrival bin, including `never`;
- `O_n(c)`: binary causal-front occupancy at cell `c`, time `n`.

## Network contract

The medium encoder produces a latent physical context:

`e_b = E_phi(G, W_b)`.

The first-passage neural field produces categorical logits over all native bins
plus `never`:

`a_theta(s, x, k, e_b) in R^(T+1)`

`pi_theta(F=n | s,x,k,W_b,G) = softmax(a_theta)_n`.

The discrete conditional arrival probability and rate are:

`alpha_skn(x) = pi_n / (pi_never + sum_(j>=n) pi_j)`

`h_skn(x) = -log(1-alpha_skn(x)) / Delta_t_n`.

This guarantees a nonnegative rate and a normalized survival contract. Empty
or never-reached transport is represented explicitly; no epsilon uniform front
is inserted.

The causal-front head predicts a normalized occupied-cell distribution only
when a front exists:

`P_theta(c | s,k,n,W_b,G) >= 0`, `sum_c P_theta(c|...) = 1`.

An explicit pre-arrival state handles an empty front. The M2 physical
transition remains:

`w_s,n(k,k') = sum_c sqrt(P_theta(c|s,k,n) P_theta(c|s,k',n+1))`,

with frozen row normalization and a zero-overlap self-loop.

## Physics restrictions

The network is a solver surrogate, not a posterior corrector. Its training loss
contains only simulator-derived terms:

`L_M1 = L_first-arrival + beta_front L_front + beta_recon L_frequency + beta_causal L_causal`.

- `L_first-arrival`: categorical NLL of the recorded first-arrival/never state;
- `L_front`: proper cross-entropy or Hellinger loss for occupied-cell fronts;
- `L_frequency`: reconstructed cumulative frequency versus the native simulator;
- `L_causal`: source boundary, obstacle mask, and pre-arrival consistency.

The following inputs and objectives are prohibited:

- real source truth;
- native PMFS posterior or candidate rank;
- final localization error;
- planner action or future trajectory;
- a learned reliability/gating weight;
- end-to-end optimization of closed-loop performance.

## Identification and split

Training examples are generated by the physical filament simulator over a
preregistered design of wind contexts, source carriers, and keyed transport
members. Splits occur by complete wind context and complete transport key, not
by individual cells from the same simulation.

At least one whole wind context and at least two transport keys are held out for
M1. House-level generalization is not claimed by a within-House split.

## M1 advance gate

On held-out physical simulations, the neural field must beat the unconditioned
V12 frequency comparator on both:

1. first-arrival categorical NLL (primary proper score);
2. integrated Brier score of arrival-by-time.

It must also satisfy:

- exact normalization including `never`;
- zero probability on obstacle query cells;
- cumulative-frequency reconstruction within a frozen tolerance;
- inference latency below the source-update budget;
- no degradation when evaluated twice under identical inputs/keys.

Only then is it evaluated on frozen real observation traces for pre-Bayes source
evidence ordering. M2/M3 and closed-loop House123 runs remain forbidden until
this direct M1 gate passes.
