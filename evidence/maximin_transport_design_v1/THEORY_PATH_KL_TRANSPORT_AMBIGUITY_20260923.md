## 1. Core change

A generic KL ball around the final hit likelihood would reduce M1 to generic DRO/robust-infotaxis.

PMFS has a stronger structure: candidate hit maps are generated by a stochastic filament transport process. Put ambiguity on that transport transition law first, then propagate it to sensing likelihood.

Working terms:
- Path-KL Transport Ambiguity (PKTA)
- Transport-Certified Information Floor (TCIF)

Names are provisional.

## 2. Official PMFS code anchor

MAPIRlab/GasSourceLocalization, humble:
gsl_server/src/gsl_server/algorithms/PMFS/internal/Simulations.cpp

The implementation forms velocity as wind plus zero-mean Gaussian noise with standard deviation noiseSTDev, then updates position by deltaTime times velocity and applies moveAlongPath.

Before deterministic obstacle/path handling:

\[
Z_{k+1}=X_k+\Delta t[u(X_k)+\varepsilon_k],
\qquad \varepsilon_k\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma_v^2I).
\]

For an admissibly misspecified drift:

\[
Z'_{k+1}=X_k+\Delta t[u(X_k)+\delta u(X_k)+\varepsilon_k].
\]

## 3. Exact one-step KL

The nominal and perturbed Gaussian proposal kernels share covariance

\[
\Sigma=(\Delta t\,\sigma_v)^2I
\]

and their means differ by \(\Delta t\,\delta u\). Hence

\[
D_{\rm KL}(Q_k\|P_k)
=
\frac12(\Delta t\,\delta u)^T\Sigma^{-1}(\Delta t\,\delta u)
=
\boxed{\frac{\|\delta u\|^2}{2\sigma_v^2}}.
\]

For PMFS as written, deltaTime cancels exactly.

Thus the statistical size of a transport error is its squared wind-drift error normalized by the filament velocity-noise variance.

## 4. Obstacle handling

moveAlongPath is deterministic conditional on the proposal. Let Phi denote that map. By data processing,

\[
D_{\rm KL}(\Phi_\#Q_k\|\Phi_\#P_k)
\le
D_{\rm KL}(Q_k\|P_k)
=
\frac{\|\delta u(X_k)\|^2}{2\sigma_v^2}.
\]

So deterministic obstacle handling cannot increase this KL bound.

## 5. Multi-step path ambiguity

For a Markov filament path, KL chain rule gives

\[
D_{\rm KL}(Q_{0:T}\|P_{0:T})
=
\sum_{k=0}^{T-1}
\mathbb E_Q[D_{\rm KL}(Q_k(\cdot|X_k)\|P_k(\cdot|X_k))].
\]

Therefore

\[
D_{\rm KL}(Q_{0:T}\|P_{0:T})
\le
\mathbb E_Q\left[
\sum_{k=0}^{T-1}
\frac{\|\delta u(X_k)\|^2}{2\sigma_v^2}
\right]
\equiv \rho.
\]

Interpret rho as a normalized drift-error-energy budget.

A final method should constrain drift perturbations to physically plausible/smooth wind-field changes rather than arbitrary independent per-cell corruption.

## 6. Push transport ambiguity to hit/miss likelihood

For source candidate s and sensing position x, PMFS provides nominal hit probability

\[
h_s(x)=P(Y_x=1|s).
\]

A hit/miss observation is downstream of the transported filament process, so data processing gives

\[
D_{\rm KL}(\mathrm{Ber}(q_s(x))\|\mathrm{Ber}(h_s(x)))
\le
D_{\rm KL}(Q_s\|P_s)
\le\rho_s(x).
\]

This induces an interval

\[
q_s(x)\in[L_s(x),U_s(x)]
\]

where

\[
d_{\rm Ber}(q,h_s(x))\le\rho_s(x).
\]

The Bernoulli KL ball is generally a conservative relaxation of the physically reachable hit probabilities. Use it for the first cheap falsification, not automatically as the final physical model.

## 7. Transport-Certified Information Floor

Let pi(s) be the current source belief. Define

\[
\underline I_\rho(x)
=
\min_{\{q_s\}} I_\pi(S;Y_x)
\]

subject to the Bernoulli-KL constraints above.

For binary Y:

\[
I_\pi(S;Y)
=
H(\bar q)-\sum_s\pi(s)H(q_s),
\qquad
\bar q=\sum_s\pi(s)q_s.
\]

Ordinary infotaxis asks how separated nominal source-conditioned likelihoods are.

TCIF asks how much source separation cannot be erased by an admissible transport error.

## 8. Binary inner adversary reduces to one scalar

Each Bernoulli KL ball is an interval [L_s,U_s].

For fixed source prior, mutual information is convex in the observation channel. KKT stationarity for an unconstrained q_s gives

\[
\operatorname{logit}(q_s)=\operatorname{logit}(\bar q).
\]

Thus the least-favorable channel has form

\[
\boxed{q_s^\star=\operatorname{clip}(c^\star,L_s,U_s)}
\]

with the scalar fixed point

\[
\boxed{
c^\star
=
\sum_s\pi(s)\operatorname{clip}(c^\star,L_s,U_s)
}.
\]

Then

\[
\underline I_\rho(x)
=
H(c^\star)-\sum_s\pi(s)H(q_s^\star).
\]

If all intervals have a common intersection, the adversary can make every source-conditioned hit probability identical and the certified information floor is zero.

This gives a cheap whole-map computation: two scalar Bernoulli-KL roots per source plus one scalar fixed point per sensing cell.

## 10. Novelty boundary

Do not claim KL-DRO, path-space relative entropy, robust sensor placement, robust active inference, Renyi/Sibson information, robust infotaxis, or multiple wrong plume models as new.

The candidate-specific contribution can only be the domain bridge:

derive transport-level ambiguity from PMFS stochastic filament physics and use its induced least-favorable source observation channel to choose mobile measurements that preserve source identity under forward-model mismatch.
