# Interpretation after D0

## If SIGNAL / STRONG_SIGNAL

Do NOT call the main innovation finished.

The evidence would mean:
- the candidate mean encounter trajectory is insufficient;
- the source-specific residual/branching law adds source identity even when the
  mean is held exactly fixed;
- a stochastic/multimodal world-model transfer becomes scientifically licensed.

Only then design a fresh confirmation set under a new pre-registered
source/wind split. The 2026 world-model theory can then motivate the model, but
the GSL-specific contribution must be the source-conditioned stochastic
forward law and its inference contract, not generic uncertainty marginalization.

## If MEAN_ONLY

This is the likely boundary:
- ensemble/marginalization can improve calibration and rankings;
- but the identity-bearing object is predominantly the stable source mean
  encounter profile;
- source-specific branching is not established.

Do not spend tokens building a stochastic world model as the paper mainline.

## If NULL_OR_ADVERSE

Stop this mainline completely.

The current evidence would then support fixing/robustifying the mean forward
representation, not modeling richer source-conditioned stochastic futures.
