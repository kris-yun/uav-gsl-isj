## Why this knife exists

The evidence chain now separates three different ideas:

1. High-order H01 joint plume structure was already falsified by
   `distributional_forward_v1`.
2. R0 showed that source-conditioned binary encounter *mean probabilities* are
   highly reproducible and source-informative on the 18-source calibration
   panel.
3. WIND_ALIGNMENT_D0 showed a `MIXTURE_ONLY` signal: marginalizing several
   transport hypotheses greatly improved the true-source proper score, but
   correct event-to-wind alignment did not beat permutation controls.

Therefore "use more uncertainty" is not yet a main innovation. The remaining
unanswered question is narrower:

> After preserving each candidate source's mean 10-step encounter trajectory
> exactly, does that source's own realization-to-realization branching/residual
> distribution carry additional source identity?

If NO, stop the stochastic-world-model mainline.  
If YES, only then justify a fresh confirmation study and a 2026 stochastic
world-model transfer.

The 18-source R0 panel is OPEN/discovery data. Any positive result here is
development evidence only.
