## Pre-registered experiment interface

When a qualifying bank is available, the exact comparison is:

1. **Frozen transfer:** the released GeoPT geometry + wind backbone is frozen;
   only a small source-injection adapter and output head are trainable.
2. **Scratch control:** the same architecture and adapter size with a random
   backbone, trained under the identical budget.
3. **Required input:** spatial free-space geometry (7-D), explicit 4-D wind
   prompt, localized source intervention, and a plume field target.
4. **Split:** fix seen source × wind cells for training and at least one unseen
   source × wind cell for testing before fitting. Do not choose the split from
   source-rank outcomes.
5. **Primary estimand:** rank of the truth-containing source candidate on the
   held-out source × wind field. Field error is auxiliary.
6. **Controls:** random GeoPT weights, source-adapter shuffle, and wind-prompt
   shuffle. A field-only improvement cannot pass G1.

The gate can advance only when the bank supplies all four required tensors and
metadata for every sample:

`geometry_7d[N,7]`, `wind_4d[N,4]`, `source_xyz[3]` (or localized `Q_s[N,*]`),
and `plume_target[N,T or C]`, plus immutable `house/source/wind/split` labels.
