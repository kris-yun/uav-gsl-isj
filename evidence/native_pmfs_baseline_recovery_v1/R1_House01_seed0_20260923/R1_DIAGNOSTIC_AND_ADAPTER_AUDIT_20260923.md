## Separate VGR/GMRF adapter audit

The VGR bridge source (`vgr_sim_node.py`, SHA256 `2b5d69d5a7cb00def5f462e1f0bc985eb9ca27a4bdad0bd3774abcd7e9204ed5`, lines 928–934) publishes `/Anemometer/WindSensor_reading` direction as `atan2(wind_v, wind_u)` in the `map` frame, i.e. the direction of its flow vector. The installed GMRF source (`gmrf_node.cpp`, SHA256 `84e4cba3dac5ed76b25f3eb86703b98db79a939eec491ba5475288bc893b4e74`, lines 220–240) explicitly interprets that sensor direction as **upwind** and adds pi to obtain downwind in `map`. Those source contracts conflict for nonzero wind. No 180-degree correction was applied in this task. The direct GADEN ground-truth service bypassed GMRF for Native PMFS. R1 GMRF binary SHA256 was `a97a118f98174c168272e61c802fac48c0968778360110fef2e6507d96aecc13`; source repository commit `2ec7a5db7bf5f2597e9d62ba662d3efcfc788d71` had deleted `gmrf_msgs` files in its working tree. The observer field was nearly zero at this snapshot, so its rank effect must not be attributed solely to a directional sign.
