## 1. Paper-level mother idea

The candidate is not "use another gas feature".

The transferred scientific principle is **turbulent universality, intermittency and multiscaling**:

> Two instantaneous turbulent scalar fields need not agree cell-by-cell in order to be generated by the same transport regime. A physically compatible source hypothesis should instead reproduce the observation's cross-scale increment statistics.

For every source hypothesis (s), compare the measured field and the source-conditioned simulated field through a fixed family of scalar structure functions

[
S_p^{(f)}(\ell)
=
\frac{\sum_{(i,j)\in E_\ell}\sqrt{c_i c_j}\,|f_j-f_i|^p}
     {\sum_{(i,j)\in E_\ell}\sqrt{c_i c_j}},
qquad
p\in\{1,2,3,4\},
]

at fixed separations (ell\in\{1,2,4,8\}) PMFS grid cells.

The local scale exponent is

[
\zeta_p^{(f)}(\ell)
=
\frac{\log S_p^{(f)}(2\ell)-\log S_p^{(f)}(\ell)}
     {\log 2}.
]

The candidate conformance score is

[
C(s)
=
-\operatorname{mean}_{p,\ell}
\left|
\zeta_p^{(m)}(\ell)
-
\zeta_p^{(q_s)}(\ell)
\right|,
]

where (m) is the measured hit-probability field and (q_s) is the candidate-conditioned simulated hit-probability field.

This changes the scientific object from **pointwise plume reproduction** to **membership in the same multiscale turbulent-scalar statistical class**.

## 2. Recent remote-field anchors

Two direct modern anchors motivate the transfer:

1. Kiran, Kumar, Gupta, Pandit & Ray, **"Onset of Intermittency and Multiscaling in Active Turbulence"**, *Physical Review Letters* 134, 088302 (2025). DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.088302. The paper uses Eulerian/Lagrangian structure functions and multiscaling as signatures of intermittent turbulent states.

2. Tang, Antonia, Djenidi & Danaila, **"Dual scaling and the 2/3 power law for the second-order scalar structure function in homogeneous turbulence with a mean scalar gradient"**, *Journal of Fluid Mechanics* 1013, A46 (2025). DOI: 10.1017/jfm.2025.10319. This is especially relevant because gas concentration is a transported scalar and the paper studies scale laws of scalar increments rather than pointwise scalar values.

Adjacent OSL literature already uses intermittency, burst timing, variance, and other plume statistics. The novelty claim therefore must **not** be "intermittency is useful for odor localization".

Targeted novelty hypothesis:

> **source inference by hypothesis-conditioned conformity of measured and simulated turbulent-scalar multiscaling laws.**

The current targeted search found no direct GSL/OSL implementation of candidate-wise structure-function exponent-spectrum conformity. This is a targeted prior-art screen, not an exhaustive novelty proof.

## 4. Frozen V1 implementation

No parameter is fitted to source truth.

Fixed choices:

- powers: (p=1,2,3,4);
- spatial separations: 1, 2, 4, 8 cells;
- directions: +x and +y cardinal pairs;
- pair weight: (sqrt{c_i c_j});
- numerical support floor: measured confidence (>10^{-6});
- missing/degenerate candidates receive zero percentile density;
- valid final-leaf scores are converted to average percentile ranks;
- every free cell inherits the rank density of its authoritative final leaf;
- endpoint uses (lfloor0.05N_{free}\rfloor) highest-density cells and the same density-weighted ExpectedValue construction as the native evaluator.

The (10^{-6}) floor is a frozen numerical-support convention, not a truth-fitted threshold. As a robustness check, including the machine-floor confidence rows still preserves 6/6 improvement and roughly 51% mean error reduction.

## 6. Destructive controls

### A. Final-leaf score permutation

Keep the exact final quadtree partition and the exact HCMC score/rank distribution, but randomly permute ranks between final leaves.

300 deterministic null repetitions:

- null mean endpoint error: **4.4958 m**
- null median: **4.5308 m**
- null 5th–95th percentile: **3.4703–5.4549 m**
- repetitions as good as or better than real HCMC overall: **1/300**

Therefore the gain is not explained only by the existing final-leaf geometry.

### B. Simulated-field spatial destruction

Within every source candidate independently, randomly permute simulated hit probabilities over the same support cells. This preserves the marginal simulated-value distribution, support, confidence field, candidate geometry, and final partition while destroying spatial increment structure.

30 deterministic repetitions:

- null mean endpoint error: **4.9613 m**
- null median: **4.9033 m**
- null 5th–95th percentile: **3.9451–5.7973 m**
- repetitions as good as or better than real HCMC overall: **0/30**

Therefore the result depends on spatial multiscale organization, not only the marginal value histogram.

### C. Cross-seed source-blind reproducibility

Spearman correlation of HCMC candidate scores on identical valid final-leaf IDs between seed0 and seed1:

- House01: **0.440**, permutation upper p ≈ **0.020**
- House02: **0.848**, permutation upper p ≈ **0.001**
- House03: **0.255**, permutation upper p ≈ **0.008**

The signal is not equally strong in every House, but it reproduces above permutation null in all three environments.

## 7. Why this currently outranks prior candidates

The previous negative/weak screens establish useful contrasts:

- pointwise/native compatibility can become 6/6 false-confident;
- generic temporal likelihood/TPSD did not improve the native 300-s endpoint;
- MIPO deliberate modulation generated signal but not source direction;
- rough-path candidate scores failed trajectory-geometry destruction controls;
- optimal-transport geometry was strong but collided directly with existing UOT source-localization literature;
- raw RG/coarse-graining drift showed a strong House03 effect but poor House01 cross-seed reproducibility;
- generalized-Bayes Brier/Hellinger/JS scores produced only small pooled improvements.

HCMC is the first current candidate that simultaneously shows:

1. a paper-level physical thesis;
2. a fixed native 300-s endpoint improvement in all six cases;
3. a large pooled effect;
4. spatial-destruction sensitivity;
5. partition-null separation;
6. source-blind cross-seed reproducibility in all three Houses;
7. no direct targeted GSL/OSL collision found so far.

## 8. Hard claim boundary

HCMC does **not** yet establish:

- a universal inertial-range exponent in these indoor VGR fields;
- a formal multifractal spectrum theorem;
- turbulence universality across arbitrary environments;
- closed-loop improvement;
- independent generalization to unseen seeds or datasets.

The paper should initially use **multiscale / multiscaling conformance** language rather than claiming a measured Kolmogorov or multifractal law.
