# CD-D0: saved Native action–sensory alignment diagnostic

## Outcome

`CD_D0_OPEN_ALIGNMENT_SIGNAL_ONLY_NOT_MAINLINE_CONFIRMATION`

Correct action–sensory pairing produces better source ranks than a source-blind permutation of the candidate template changes in the existing H01/H02 OPEN Native trajectories. This is a development observation. It does not show that a corollary-discharge mechanism has been isolated from ordinary spatial template consistency, and it does not establish a new closed-loop algorithm.

`rawu-CD` is not consistently stronger than `u-CD`: H01 improves in mean source rank, but H02 worsens. The AOD identity contribution required by the proposed dual-stream architecture is therefore not established by this D0. No PMFS planner repair, dual-stream probability map, or closed-loop implementation was started.

## Frozen calculation

- 49 existing H01/H02 Native trajectories: H01 16 from four physical sources; H02 33 from five physical sources. House03 is excluded because its one saved Native truth lies outside the Native 615 support. This exclusion was in the pre-score protocol.
- Five deployed measurement events at the same exact robot pose form one completed stop. The stop readout is their mean measured concentration. Consecutive completed stops yield real `dy` and source-template `dm` values. The same nonnegative gain-profile B2 SSE and legal candidate support are used for `u` and `rawu`.
- For each trajectory, 200 fixed-seed, source-blind permutations break the association between the actual concentration change and the candidate template change. Both arms receive the same permutations. Ordinary absolute-level B2 on the same stops is a comparator.
- Main descriptive endpoint is the final Native source-update prefix. Earlier update prefixes are saved for audit, never counted as independent trajectories. Results are aggregated by physical source before House.

Real CD yields zero unique Top-1 among H01 physical-source averaged cases for either arm. In H02, `u-CD` has a source-weighted unique Top-1 rate of 0.025 and `rawu-CD` zero. Large rank improvements relative to a broken pairing therefore do **not** amount to reliable source localization.

## Verification and interpretation limit

- The ordinary B2 rank and unique Top-1 computed after five-event stop averaging matched the D1 per-event B2 on all 98 arm-by-trajectory comparisons. The corresponding best-wrong SSE margins matched after the exact factor of five induced by repeating one template five times per stop.
- Full CD scoring repeated byte-identically.
- The destructive permutation deliberately mismatches observed changes with spatial template changes. Its failure can reflect basic location-conditioned template information; it does not, by itself, distinguish self-motion reafference from transport-process stochasticity.
- This is OPEN data inspected after D1. The source document did not supply a numeric pre-data gate, and the present test cannot be promoted into independent confirmation. A future test would need a frozen causal control and a new held-out dataset before calling the proposed corollary-discharge architecture a main innovation.
