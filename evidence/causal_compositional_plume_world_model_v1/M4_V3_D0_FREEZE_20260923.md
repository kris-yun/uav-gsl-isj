## Spatial/time contract

- 2x spatial reduction, matching the v2 development comparison.
- Effective grid spacing after reduction: `0.2 m`.
- Internal transport step: `0.1 s`.
- GADEN wind timing is recreated with float32 current-time semantics and the same update order:
  MoveFilaments first, then wind-index update, then current-time increment.
- Wind iteration period: `1.0 s`.
- Loop: iteration 1 through 10 after the initial iteration 0.

The 0.1 s step is frozen because the observed House02 maximum wind is above 1 m/s; a 1 s characteristic step would cross many 0.2 m cells and make wall handling scientifically indefensible.

## Frozen M4-v3 structure

Implementation:
`research/causal_compositional_plume_world_model_v1/m4_v3_interventional_evolution.py`

Method state:
1. source is injected as a separate nonnegative forcing field;
2. explicit semi-Lagrangian backtrace uses the local W(t);
3. a 5-point local closure is conditioned only on wind, with positive normalized weights;
4. invalid obstacle neighbours are masked before normalization;
5. transport has no source ID, source coordinates, or source-conditioning path;
6. the mapping remains linear in the source/concentration state for fixed wind/geometry;
7. a small source-independent sink is permitted for unresolved vertical/outlet loss.

The diagnostic `characteristic_scale=0` switch is an ablation only and is never trained as part of the method.
