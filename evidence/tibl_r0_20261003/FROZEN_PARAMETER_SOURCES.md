## Fixed assumptions, not measured quantities

theta_ref=300 K; g=9.81 m/s²; u=2 m/s; w=0; Kx=.5 m²/s; x-z domain 1000x150 m. Source-x truths 73.4,161.7,287.3 m; source height 3 m; smooth source widths 5/.75 m. The 3 m source is an illustrative near-ground release, not a reconstruction of the unavailable Wilmington experiment. Q=1 arbitrary mass/alongshore-length/s, baseline .002 in consistent arbitrary concentration units, Gaussian white sensor noise sigma .0005. No methane ppm calibration is claimed.

Kz outside h is zero. The model lacks entrainment, ambient stable-layer turbulence, nonlocal convective mass flux and molecular diffusion. Equal diffusivity for the passive tracer and heat is an unvalidated Schmidt/Prandtl assumption. These omissions bound any outcome: a positive result concerns the prescribed mathematical family; a STOP does not falsify all real TIBL physics.

## Matched controls and observation design

Z_MEAN is the x-average of TIBL Kz on the same grid and physical domain; K_MEAN is its whole-domain mean. Their integral budgets must match before scoring. Identical u/w/Kx/source/Q/background/boundaries/duration/path/noise/clock apply. Same u matches advective travel time, not an unmeasured full residence-time distribution.

Two fixed paths: three vertical curtains at (400,700,900) m or (500,800,950) m, heights 2..102 m in 2 m increments. This is a synthetic fixed-route inverse problem; no autonomous discovery or UAV mission feasibility is claimed. All three inverse models use EXACTLY the same TIBL observations, so their detection opportunity and SNR cannot differ. Additionally, a predeclared common subset uses all-three-world expected enhancement >=3sigma and max/min SNR ratio<=1.25, preserving identical receptor positions and counts. This is truth-informed synthetic evaluation, not a deployable data-selection algorithm. If fewer than 24 samples remain, the matched analysis is unassessable; do not call it proof of a detection confound.

The source likelihood profiles nonnegative Q and an unrestricted additive background for every x. Dense 1 m likelihood curves are interpolated and optimized continuously for the point estimate, instead of a nine-source classifier. Profile intervals use delta chi²<=3.84146 with known noise; these are likelihood intervals, not Bayesian credible intervals. Source-template x spans 20..350 m at 10 m knots. Boundary optima and coverage are reported. Model-aware recovery is partly an inverse-consistency check, not independent field skill; independent fine-grid forward checks bound discretization error.

## Frozen operational gates

These numerical thresholds are engineering choices made before inspecting new results, not literature truths. Primary grid 5x1.25 m; coarse 10x2.5; fine 2.5x.625. Numerical quality requires <=5% receptor-profile relative L2 against fine/top/outlet tests and finite-duration time stepping, nonnegativity and mass discrepancy<=1e-8. The coarse grid is a convergence diagnostic, not a requirement that a deliberately coarse mesh already be accurate. Integrate implicit time steps 20 and 10 s to fixed 1500 s for the independent transient check before accepting stationary-limit profiles. Boundary sensitivity includes taller/further boxes, absorbing top and a separately disclosed absorbing ground stress test.

Geometry stage: both matched controls must have normalized observation-profile JSD>=.01, and the JSD residual must exceed five times the same-condition numerical JSD envelope. No geometry-only PASS.

Source stage: after profiling Q/background, each incorrect inverse must have median absolute bias>=20 m, median correct-aware error<=5 m, and >=50% recovery; at least 75% of paired trials must show recovery. The sign of mismatch median bias must agree across the two K forms for the primary heat flux. Require both fixed paths, multiple sources and seeds. Repeat using the same-position matched-detection subset. Heat-flux .04 must not reverse the primary bias direction. Boundary-hit likelihood optima cannot count as positive evidence. Ground absorption is a different physical model and is reported separately; if effects depend on unverified ground handling, HOLD rather than promote.
