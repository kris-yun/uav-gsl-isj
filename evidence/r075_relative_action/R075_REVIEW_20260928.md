# R0.75 relative-versus-action mechanism audit

## Decision

`R075_STOP_ACTION_SPECIFIC_MAINLINE_RELATIVE_CODING_SUFFICIENT_IN_OPEN`

The existing 49 H01/H02 Native trajectories support a **relative-level** explanation for most of the CD-D0 rank improvement. At the House physical-source aggregation level, mean-centering the stop observations and every candidate template improves truth rank beyond ordinary absolute B2 and performs better than the actual consecutive-stop difference in H01 and H02, for both `u` and `rawu`. The actual flight order does not consistently beat a joint permutation that keeps each observation matched to its real position and candidate template. The proposed action-equivariant or corollary-discharge mainline is therefore stopped at this OPEN audit. This does not refute all possible action-conditioned inference; it rejects the current simple adjacent-difference mechanism under this frozen comparison.

The centered result is also not a new main innovation. It is an OPEN diagnostic, source ranks are still often poor, and the simple offset/common-mode interpretation requires future independent testing before a method claim.

## Frozen comparison

The pre-score protocol was committed as `e246493036589ae820c5275f24db1a51ce31f751`. It reused exactly the 49 H01/H02 Native trajectories, complete legal candidate supports, saved `u/rawu` model banks, 0.20 m footprint, nonnegative gain-profile SSE, and source-update prefixes from D1/CD-D0. Five real measurement events per completed stop became one stop mean; the two arms saw identical observations. Only final complete source updates are scientific trajectory endpoints; 176 earlier/final update records are available for diagnostics.

`ABS` fits absolute stop levels; `CENTERED` subtracts the mean of the observation and each candidate template; `REAL_ADJ` fits the actual sequence of adjacent stop differences; `JOINT_SHUFFLED_ADJ` fits 200 source-blind permutations of **stop order**, applying each permutation jointly to observation and every candidate template. Unlike CD-D0's old null, this preserves the observation–position–template pairing. Ranking is over every Native-legal candidate. Truth is used only after candidate scores have been calculated.

The mean rank direction favors CENTERED in all four House × arm cells. It is **not** a claim that every physical source benefits: several sources favor REAL_ADJ, and those cases are preserved in `R075_RESULT.json`. Under the jointly shuffled control, real order is worse in H01 `u`, approximately tied in H01 `rawu`, and modestly better in H02. There is no stable two-House action-order increment.

Best-wrong SSE margin, unique Top-1, and Top-3 are recorded for every source and update. Raw SSE margins from different transforms have different observation energy and should not be compared as if they shared a scale. In the House-level summaries, CENTERED does not create a broad Top-1 recovery: all four CENTERED cells have zero source-weighted unique Top-1. The relative encoding is a mechanistic lead, not a solved localization system.

## Checks and limits

- 704 arm × update × method checks: R0.75 ABS and REAL_ADJ rank, unique Top-1, and best-wrong margin match the previously frozen CD-D0 output exactly.
- Independent numerical test confirms the gain-profile all-pairs identity: all pair differences have SSE exactly `n` times the centered SSE, so a fifth all-pairs arm is unnecessary.
- Joint stop permutation leaves absolute and centered candidate scores invariant in an independent numerical test.
- Repeating the complete R0.75 computation yielded a byte-identical result JSON.
- The 200 permutations are within-trajectory controls, not 200 independent scientific units. H02 repeated winds are aggregated within the same physical source. H03 is unavailable for a within-Native-support rank comparison and was excluded before scoring.
- No VGR, new plume, PMFS repair, probability map, network, or closed-loop method was run.
