# DS-PMFS Identity D1: fixed Native trajectory shadow readout

## Decision

`DS_PMFS_IDENTITY_D1_STOP_NO_STABLE_CROSS_HOUSE_GAIN`

The requested first-stage dual-stream premise is not supported on the saved Native trajectories. This is a read-only development analysis, not a new closed-loop campaign. The earlier AOD F1 result remains historical evidence under its own fixed path and candidate contract.

The source-level comparison is mixed. House01 has 2 improved and 2 harmed physical sources. House02 has 3 improved and 2 harmed physical sources when its two winds are first aggregated within each source; its equal-physical-source mean rank changes from 75.30 (`u`) to 75.75 (`rawu`). House03 has only one saved Native trajectory. Its truth is excluded from the Native 615 support, so the House03 shadow scores use the frozen F1 624 support for **both** arms. This one case changes rank 6 to 5 and cannot establish stable cross-House behavior.

No identity probability map, fusion weight, temperature, new action value, planner change, network, PMFS forward, plume, or VGR campaign was run.

## Frozen inputs and method

- Pre-score asset freeze: `ASSET_FREEZE.json`, committed before rank computation. It contains exact hashes for 48 original Native `.tar.zst` archives, two more complete saved Native trajectories, the three H01/H02 legal-support banks, House03 F1 templates, and occupancy masks.
- 50 trajectories: H01 16, H02 33, H03 1. H01/H02 use complete Native legal source supports of 596 and 630. H03 uses the F1 624 shadow support because its truth is outside Native615. The Native Action Map and trajectory are untouched in all cases.
- Each deployed `measurement_events.csv` concentration is checked against `measurement_blocks.csv`. The 48 archived episodes additionally verify raw file hashes against their frozen episode metadata and position parity against the encoded episodes. The scorer uses every already completed event up to Native source-update prefixes 20, 35, 50, or 65. Five readings from one stop remain one trajectory, never five scientific replicates.
- Both amplitude channels use the same 0.20 m by 0.20 m physical footprint and the archived B2 nonnegative gain-profile SSE with `EPS=1e-9`. Lower SSE wins. `u` on H01/H02 is deterministically reconstructed from the saved full-support `rawu` mean maps using the frozen OpenCV Gaussian blur and occupancy-mask normalization. House03 uses saved F1 `u` and `rawu` arrays. An independent reconstruction of five House03 `u` maps agrees with the saved array to maximum absolute `1.83e-5` under its own frozen occupancy mask. H01/H02 do not retain full-support saved `u` arrays for direct per-candidate parity.
- Truth is used only after the same candidate SSE vectors have been calculated; neither shadow stream affects actual movement or Native source probability.

## Verification and limits

- All 360 arm-by-update ranks, unique Top-1 flags, Top-3 flags and best-wrong SSE margins were recomputed directly from saved all-candidate score vectors: PASS.
- Repeated full scoring produced byte-identical `D1_RESULT.json`, `COMPACT_EVENTS.json`, and `ALL_CANDIDATE_SCORES.json`.
- The archive includes per-update and final trajectory records, source and House summaries, all-candidate SSE/gain vectors, compact deployed observations, the frozen manifest, code, and necessary model templates. Original raw trajectory archives remain at their hashed host paths.
- This D1 has no predeclared numerical noninferiority margin. The STOP is based on the user's explicit requirement for stable cross-House noninferiority and improvement: mixed source directions, House02 physical-source mean rank worsening, Top-3 losses, and only one atypical House03 case do not meet that requirement. The saved data cannot establish a stable House03 estimate.
