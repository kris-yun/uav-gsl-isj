## 1. Correction to the prior postmortem narrative

The earlier qualitative story that the local source-identifiability geometry simply 'rotates with wind direction' was overinterpreted.

Direct package re-audit shows:
- W0 split-averaged x-edge energy > y-edge energy;
- W1 split-averaged x-edge energy > y-edge energy;
- W2 is approximately isotropic, with only a small split-averaged y>x difference;
- W2 A and B do not give a stable orientation reversal.

Therefore the WCIG FAIL correctly rejects the simple local-wind orientation story.

Do not use that story in the paper or future route selection.

## 2. What WCIG actually tested and killed

WCIG R0 tested whether source-local wind summaries at the two source endpoints and midpoint, at z=0.2 m, are sufficient to predict cross-wind source-pair distinguishability.

Primary covariates were:
- along-edge local mean wind;
- cross-edge local mean wind;
- local mean speed.

The leave-one-wind-out model failed:
- pooled rho ~= -0.008;
- W0 rho ~= +0.564;
- W1 rho ~= +0.152;
- W2 rho ~= -0.527.

Thus **source-local wind summaries are not a portable predictor of inverse source-identifiability geometry** under the frozen protocol.

## 3. Why this failure is structurally informative

### 3.1 Nonlocality

The frozen 8-source cross-wind panel spans only about 0.9 m x 0.3 m.
The 30 observation probes span about 6.8 m x 10.0 m; their median distance from the source-panel center is about 4.05 m.

Therefore source identifiability is generated by a nonlocal source->transport->sensor map.

Sampling wind only at the source endpoints/midpoint discards:
- downstream advection history;
- obstacle interactions;
- vertical transport;
- sensor/probe geometry;
- time-of-flight and intermittent plume structure.

### 3.2 Environment-level pseudoreplication

WCIG has 30 edge×wind rows, but only **three independent wind operators**.

Edges inside one wind share the same environment/operator; they are not thirty independent environment samples.

Learning an environment->identifiability law from three winds is underdetermined regardless of how many plume seeds are available inside each wind.

### 3.3 Within-environment replication is not cross-environment evidence

R0/D1R rigorously stabilized realization-level statistics for one operator.
That does not estimate the distribution over operators/houses.

The project repeatedly accumulated `N_realization` while the desired claim requires `N_environment`.

### 3.4 Proxy promotion has repeatedly been too fast

Across failed routes, a proxy often showed a real local signal:
- field MSE;
- source rank on two sources;
- shared covariance;
- local stochastic distance;
- local wind alignment.

Then the proxy was promoted to a causal/mechanistic interpretation before environment-level falsification.

Future routes must reverse this order.

## 4. New hard governance

Effective immediately:

1. **No main-innovation discovery on a single environment.**
2. **No mechanism promotion from realization split alone.**
3. **N_environment is the primary sample size for generalization claims.**
4. **No new theory/model is selected from House02/W0 outcomes alone.**
5. **Environment-level discovery and validation splits are frozen before looking for mechanisms.**
6. **No local physical summary is promoted unless it predicts held-out environments.**
7. **No full 3D hidden simulator state may be a required real-flight runtime input unless a realistic estimator is defined.**
8. **Dense target-environment source banks are evaluation assets only, never a deployment requirement.**
