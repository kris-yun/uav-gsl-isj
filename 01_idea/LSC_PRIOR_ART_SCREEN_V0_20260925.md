This is a targeted search result, not proof of global novelty.

## Strongest contemporary theory anchor

Aguilar, Muñoz & Azaele, Physical Review X 16, 031015 (2026),
'Limits of Inference in Complex Systems: When Stochastic Models Become Indistinguishable'.

This work establishes a broad complex-systems framework in which finite sampling resolution and dataset size determine whether distinct stochastic models can be empirically distinguished.

The GSL mapping under audit is:
- competing stochastic model -> neighboring candidate source;
- stochastic observations -> plume realizations under the frozen observation protocol;
- model distinguishability -> source-cell distinguishability;
- finite measurement resolution -> PMFS source-identifiability limit.

## Novelty boundary

Not novel alone:
- Bhattacharyya distance;
- Chernoff/Hellinger/Le Cam theory;
- Bayesian source likelihood;
- source confusion;
- spatial graph smoothing.

Potential novelty can only be the integrated GSL formulation and inference method built around a **measured local stochastic source-distinguishability geometry** that is validated across independent plume realizations and ultimately cross-environment / real-flight conditions.
