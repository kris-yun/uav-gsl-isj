## Why the project moved here

Multiple inference-layer ideas failed to recover stable full-support source identity. The latest source-blind observability-route experiment tested 12 precommitted House02 routes with unchanged sensor physics and found 0/12 passing routes. The best route remained the historical route. In `W_slow`, the four-source response collapsed to rank 2, and `S_k01` / `S_k22` had zero exposure on the best route. Thus route ranking, posterior repair, fixed dual-view difference and simple information-gain ideas cannot create missing finite-horizon physical transport support.

The 5 s dual-receiver mismatch is not a plume-advection mechanism candidate: motion revisit occurs on roughly 4–6 s scales whereas median wind-advection scales are roughly 104–475 s.
